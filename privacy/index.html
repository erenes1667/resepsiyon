Enes Personal: privacy

Enes Personal is a private tool. Only its owner, Enes Eren, uses it, to read and send mail from his own Gmail account on his own server. It is not offered to anyone else.

  • It reads and sends only the owner's own mail.
  • Mail content stays on the owner's own server. It is used only to track replies to his own messages.
  • No data is sold, shared or used for ads.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
